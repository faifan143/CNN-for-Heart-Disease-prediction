🎯 When Each Army Wins

🏰 The Royal Decree of Choice:

🛡️ Deploy Traditional ML when: You need fast, explainable decisions for clinical practice, have limited computational resources, want proven reliability, or need to justify decisions to doctors and patients.


🧠 Deploy Deep Learning when: You prioritize maximum accuracy, have abundant computational resources, can work with "black box" decisions, or are conducting research where small accuracy gains matter.

🎭 The Ultimate Verdict

Both Empires Are Mighty, But...

🏥 For Real Medical Practice: Traditional ML Wins

Doctors need explainable, fast, reliable decisions they can trust and defend


🔬 For Research & Maximum Accuracy: Deep Learning Wins

When every percentage point of accuracy matters and interpretability can be sacrificed


👑 THE TRUTH: Context is King!
