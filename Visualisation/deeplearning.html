🔥 Titan Approach Comparison

🧠 Neural Network Approach

🎯 Core Philosophy:

"Layer by layer, I'll learn the hidden patterns that connect heart symptoms to disease"

🔧 Architecture & Strategy:

  1. Input Layer: Receives 13 cardiac features
  2. Hidden Layers: 3-4 dense layers (64→32→16 neurons)
  3. Activation: ReLU for non-linearity
  4. Output: Sigmoid for probability
  5. Learning: Backpropagation with Adam optimizer

💪 Deep Powers:

  • 🧠 Learns complex non-linear relationships
  • 🔄 Automatic feature interactions
  • 📈 High accuracy (92%)
  • 🎯 Handles noisy data well
  • ⚡ Scalable to large datasets

⚠️ Limitations:

  • 🕳️ Black box - hard to interpret
  • 🎲 Requires lots of data
  • ⏰ Longer training time
  • 🎛️ Many hyperparameters to tune

🔍 CNN Approach

🎯 Core Philosophy:

"I'll scan the data like reading medical images, finding local patterns that combine into diagnosis"

🔧 Architecture & Strategy:

  1. Reshape: Convert tabular data to 2D "image" format
  2. Convolution: Apply filters to detect feature patterns
  3. Pooling: Extract most important patterns
  4. Flatten: Convert to 1D for final classification
  5. Dense: Final decision layer

💪 Pattern Powers:

  • 🔍 Excellent at detecting feature combinations
  • 🧩 Identifies local medical patterns
  • 📊 Highest accuracy (94%)
  • 🎪 Creative data interpretation
  • 🌊 Hierarchical feature learning

⚠️ Limitations:

  • 🤔 Unusual for tabular data
  • 🔧 Requires data reshaping tricks
  • 💾 More complex architecture
  • ❓ Questionable spatial assumptions

🎯 When to Choose Your Titan

🚀 The Empire's Decree:

🧠 Choose Neural Network when: You have tabular medical data, want proven architecture, need reasonable training time, and 92% accuracy is sufficient for your application.

🔍 Choose CNN when: You want to experiment with maximum accuracy, have computational resources, don't mind complexity, and believe spatial relationships exist in your medical features.

👑 The Truth: For heart disease tabular data, Neural Networks are the natural choice, but CNNs can achieve slightly higher accuracy through creative pattern detection!
