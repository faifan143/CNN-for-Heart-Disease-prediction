About Heart Disease Prediction

This tool uses multiple machine learning models to predict the likelihood of heart disease based on patient data. The prediction is based on several key risk factors:

  • Age and Gender: Risk increases with age, and males generally have higher risk.
  • Chest Pain Type: The nature and severity of chest pain can indicate heart problems.
  • Blood Pressure and Cholesterol: High values are significant risk factors.
  • Blood Sugar: Diabetes can increase risk of heart disease.
  • ECG Results: Abnormalities can indicate existing heart damage.
  • Maximum Heart Rate: Lower maximum heart rates can indicate cardiovascular issues.
  • Exercise-Induced Angina: Chest pain during exercise is a significant indicator.

Note: This tool is for educational purposes only and should not replace professional medical advice. Always consult with healthcare professionals for diagnosis and treatment.
